# LEGAL

Legal counsel on licensing questions for the template and the projects made from it. It has
no repository here; messages reach it through the architect, who delivers them and records
the answers in `incoming/`.

What Legal would decide, and the template does not: whether third-party text may be
redistributed, under what terms, and what attribution or notice that requires. The template
can only require that such text be declared and that the declared attribution be present
(BPO evaluation response, §6 point 2).

No Legal correspondent exists. The one request sent here, T9, was answered by the
architect, who adopted the template's behaviour as policy and accepted the risk (template
decision 33); `outgoing/2026-11-06-t9-policy-adopted.md` closes it.

# BPOTEST

The BPO Testing Team: the team that ported the Business Process Ontology onto the template,
evaluated the template against that port, and runs the final evaluation (WP6). Its working
repository is `Skreen5hot/BPO-Template-Port`, which is private; nothing from it is copied
here, and this record links nothing into it. Messages reach the team through the architect,
who delivers them and records the answers in `incoming/`.

What the team decides, and the template does not: the protocol of the final evaluation, its
measurements, and its verdicts. BPO's own findings and corrections are the BPO team's, under
BPO's governance.

## The template's reading

The architect accepted our reading of your results (decision 27; the analysis is the second
reference). The template is an effective detector of structural defects and an effective
reproducibility frame. It is not a substitute for referent-level review. Over-claiming
relations, homonyms, duplicates and misclassified referents each needed a person, as the
BPO architect's framing said they would.

## What the template does next

The architect's disposition (decision 29) turned our fourteen proposed changes into a
prioritized backlog (fifth reference, "After WP6"). Twelve are approved and two are
rejected: an automatic over-claiming or homonym verdict in the gate, and loosening a check
to remove false alarms. Items 1 to 4 are the first release after WP6, and all four are now
on the template's `main` at `d89902d`:

1. **P-B2.** A rung that cannot be evaluated makes `validate.py` exit 2 in every mode. The
   summary names it on an `INCOMPLETE` line, its findings are left out of the count, and
   the report's `findings.total` is null. The run that gave 493 findings for 1,829 would
   now fail as incomplete.
2. **P-B1.** Each English definition is judged on its own. After "a", "an" or "the", it
   must begin with the parent's label as a whole word, and the warning quotes the
   definition it is about. Both of your cases are now reported: the contradicting second
   definition, and a parent named only in the differentia.
3. **Deprecation.** A deprecated class with no definition, or with no named parent, is a
   violation whose message names the deprecation as the cause. This is the regression the
   general rules caught for you.
4. **Over-claiming.** A class under realizable entity with an existential restriction on
   has realization (`obo:BFO_0000054`), or on a subproperty of it, is a warning: it says
   every instance is realized. A person decides whether that is meant.

These four are being released as template **v0.2.0**. Two of them change what a port
sees. Register entries for the old genus message go stale and have to be re-proposed. And
a definition written "An X is a G that ..." now gets a warning of its own, as do
co-asserted anchor parents (backlog item 8).

The classification field (P-A7), transitive pins (P-A1) and the drift report over shapes and
config (P-A3) come next. So does your state manifest and comparison by identity, which is
item 11, after dependency locking.

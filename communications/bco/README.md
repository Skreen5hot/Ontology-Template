# BCO

The Business Capability Ontology team. It builds the Business Capability Ontology, a module
of the Business Process Ontology, in `Skreen5hot/Business-and-Agent-Capabilities-Ontology`.
It started from an early version of the template's structure, and its code there is also
`BCO`. Messages reach the team through the architect, who delivers them and records the
answers in `incoming/`.

What the team decides, and the template does not: how BCO is built, and whether it adopts
anything the template proposes. The template does not change BCO's repository.

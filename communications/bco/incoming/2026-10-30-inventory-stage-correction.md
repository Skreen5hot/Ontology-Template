# Correction: the inventory is Stage 01 source evidence

BCO's answer (`BCO-EXAMPLE-2026-10-30-process-proposals-answer`) and the process account
you recorded both place the Capability Term Inventory in Stage 03. That is wrong by BCO's
own record, and this message corrects it.

## What was said

- The answer called the inventory Stage 03's "input" (on Stage 03's README), and said the
  rights work cost more "in BCO's Stage 03" than anything else.
- The process account showed Stage 03 (Taxonomy) as in progress, with the inventory's
  phases listed under it.

## What the record says

The inventory's scope approval, of 2026-10-27, keeps "Stage 01 source evidence, Stage 03
taxonomy development, and Stage 05 ontology formalization" separate. The inventory is
**Stage 01 source evidence**: what other catalogs say a capability is, entry by entry with
its source. **Stage 03 has not started.** No taxonomy work exists, its standard has not
been supplied, and the inventory's later phases (I-3 to I-5) are not authorized.

BCO's Stage 03 README now says so: status not started, with a pointer to the inventory
as the evidence the taxonomy will draw on, and the statement that no inventory entry is a
term of the stage until a decision admits it.

## What changes in the answer

Nothing in its substance. The rights findings stand: they were costs of gathering source
evidence, not of building a taxonomy. That strengthens the case for a rights and
provenance record (P8): it belongs to source research, before any lane or stage that
consumes the evidence. The lanes point also stands, with this placement: the inventory is
evidence beside the decision path, and a taxonomy lane consumes it only through admission.

Read the answer's two Stage 03 references as references to the inventory, which is
Stage 01 source evidence.
